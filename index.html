FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does CodeVira offer?

CodeVira offers mobile app development, game development, web development and design, SEO, App Store Optimization (ASO), digital marketing, QA and software testing, Amazon and e-commerce services, and AI solutions such as chatbots and automation.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. While we are based in Punjab, Pakistan, we work remotely with startups and businesses worldwide, including the UK, USA, Europe, the Middle East and Australia, and we adapt to your time zone for meetings.

How do I get a quote for my project?

Simply fill in the contact form, email us or message us on WhatsApp with a short description of your project. We reply within 24 hours with questions or a free estimate.

How do you price projects?

We offer fixed-price quotes for well-defined projects and flexible monthly or hourly plans for ongoing work such as marketing, SEO and maintenance. There are never hidden costs.

Will I own the source code?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, you receive full ownership of the source code, designs and related assets.

Do you provide support after launch?

Absolutely. We offer post-launch support, maintenance plans and growth services so your product keeps improving after it goes live.
